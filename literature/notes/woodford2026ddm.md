# Woodford 2026 Lecture 5

Source: `../Lecture_Slides_export-2/4850 lecture5 2026.pdf`, relative to the
project root. Title page: Michael Woodford, September 23, 2026.

Slide 10 (PDF page 17) gives the symmetric-threshold choice probability
`exp(alpha*C)/(exp(alpha*C)+exp(-alpha*C))`, with `alpha=kB/sigma^2`, and mean
time `(A/C)*tanh(alpha*C)+t0`, with `A=B/k`. Slide 11 explains that the same
alpha fixes the shapes of both choice and response-time functions. Slides
24–26 apply the model to food choice with drift proportional to the value
difference. These are the report's main theoretical sources.

The analysis treats bids as values as instructed; the lecture does not
establish that this assumption holds for these particular data. The report
does not claim to identify the drift, threshold, and noise separately.
